# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background,
experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give feedback about the work, never the person.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Prioritize accessibility and inclusive representation in visual decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information,
and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact described
in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports in good faith
and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be expanded before
the first stable release.
